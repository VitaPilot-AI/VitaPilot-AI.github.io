How SolveMate is different

  • Diagnose before matching

    A short set of questions turns a vague symptom into likely causes, success measures, a realistic budget and the expertise needed. SolveMate reviews it.

  • Both sides choose

    You see two or three available experts who fit your problem. They see a qualified problem. You're introduced only when both say yes.

  • Evidence for this problem

    Each option shows a comparable past project and what we don't know yet — not just a résumé or a star rating.

  • We run the engagement

    An engagement lead agrees milestones with you and steps in when delivery slips, so you spend less time managing.

  • Learn from results

    Every finished project records the problem, the fix, the team and the measured result, so the next recommendation is better.

How a match happens

  1. 1

    Describe the symptom

    Say what's wrong in plain words — “our support costs are growing.” A few questions later, you see the likely cause and how success will be measured.

  2. 2

    See your best options

    Two or three experts who fit, each with a comparable project and what's uncertain. Experts see your qualified problem in their list.

  3. 3

    Both say yes

    Each side says “Interested” or “not a fit” with a reason. A match opens a short scoping call, not a stream of proposals.

  4. 4

    Agree a plan

    Milestones, a fixed price and the measure of success. An engagement lead coordinates delivery and steps in if it slips.

  5. 5

    Measure the result

    Weeks after go-live the result is recorded with its limits. It becomes evidence that improves the next match.
