What we read in your problem

Five things decide whether an expert is a fit. We find all five in your words.

  • Scope

    What's actually broken, how big it is, and what "solved" looks like.

  • Expertise

    The specific kind of problem, not a job title. "Pricing-driven churn," not "consultant."

  • Budget

    What you can spend, so every introduction is someone you can actually hire.

  • Timeline

    When you need results and who's free to start then.

  • Working style

    Embedded or advisory, hands-on or strategic, async or in the room.

How an engagement runs

From problem to outcome, we coordinate every step.

You talk to us about the problem. We handle the parts between that and a finished result.

  1. 1

    Describe

    Send the problem the way you'd explain it to a colleague. No RFP, no job spec.

  2. 2

    Scope

    We ask the right follow-up questions and turn it into a clear brief you approve.

  3. 3

    Match

    We source experts on and off our network, reach out, and introduce you to a short list of strong fits.

  4. 4

    Agree

    We handle pricing and contracts so both sides can start quickly.

  5. 5

    Deliver

    We stay with the project through execution and delivery, and check the outcome at the end.

One expert, or a small team

Some problems need one person. Some need three who've never met.

When no single expert covers the whole problem, we put together a small team — say, a pricing strategist with a data analyst — and coordinate them as one engagement with one contract.
