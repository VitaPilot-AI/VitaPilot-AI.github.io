    How to use it

    Review first. Choose only the fixes you need.

    1. 01

      Inspect locally

      Pick a CSV or TSV. The tool checks structure, blanks, whitespace, duplicate rows, formula-like values, and date-style differences in this tab.

    2. 02

      Choose reversible rules

      Decide whether to trim, skip blank rows, remove exact duplicates, or protect formula-like values. Original source rows stay in memory until you clear the file or close the tab.

    3. 03

      Export evidence

      Download a separate CSV and JSON summary. Conflicting or unclear data is flagged for human review instead of being guessed.
