Before you send a file.

Can I do it myself for free?

Yes. CSV Flight Check runs locally in your browser. It checks a CSV or TSV, previews your chosen cleanup rules, and exports a separate CSV and issue report. The paid service adds a reviewed Excel delivery and a change log.

What counts as a duplicate?

An entire source row with the same values in every column. We keep its first occurrence. We do not merge records based on a shared name, ID, or approximate match. Trimming two different rows into the same values does not authorize deleting one.

Will you fix dates, formulas, or missing values?

We flag ambiguous or missing values. We do not guess dates or invent values. The $15 package does not repair formulas or accept formula-bearing workbooks. Export a values-only copy or ask about a separate scope.

How are my data and AI tools handled?

This page has no upload and uses no analytics. Your request stays in this tab until you share it. Paid work is AI-assisted where agreed, with local spreadsheet checks and review. Send only non-sensitive data you are authorized to share, and tell us any restrictions on AI use before we accept. Email and payment are handled by their respective providers. We agree the delivery channel and file-retention terms in writing before receiving a file.

What if the file is bigger or the work is not a fit?

Request a quote first; do not pay $15 for a larger file. If the scope cannot be accepted, no work starts and no payment is due. If the delivery misses the agreed rules, ask for the included correction before accepting it.
